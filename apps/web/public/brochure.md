# AMGI Asset & License Management
**Your Centralized SaaS Solution for Enterprise IT Asset Lifecycle & Compliance**

---

## 🚀 Overview

Managing software licenses and hardware assets across multiple branches and departments is complex, error-prone, and costly. The **AMGI Asset & License Management** module is a powerful, cloud-based platform designed to centralize, track, and optimize your organization's IT infrastructure.

Whether you are tracking perpetual software keys, SaaS subscription renewals, or physical hardware lifecycles, our platform provides a single pane of glass for all your IT procurement and maintenance needs.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 💻 Hardware Asset Tracking
Track the complete lifecycle of all physical assets—from End-User Devices (Laptops, Mobile) to Network Equipment and Servers.
*   **Asset Allocation:** Assign assets to specific users, departments, and branches.
*   **Lifecycle & Maintenance:** Track Purchase Date, Warranty Periods, AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts), and scheduled maintenance.
*   **Depreciation & Disposal:** Monitor purchase costs, current depreciated value, and secure disposal tracking.

### 2. 🔑 Software & License Management
Eliminate unused licenses and stay compliant with our comprehensive software tracking.
*   **Subscription vs Perpetual:** Track all types of licenses including Cloud Services (AWS/Azure), Productivity Tools (M365/Google), and ERPs.
*   **Seat Management:** Monitor purchased vs. assigned seats to optimize spending.
*   **Compliance Monitoring:** Detect unauthorized usage and track compliance status to avoid vendor penalties.

### 3. 🏢 Supplier & Vendor Management
Keep all vendor interactions, SLAs, and contracts in one place.
*   **Contract Management:** Track start/end dates and SLA details.
*   **Escalation Matrix:** Maintain support contact details and escalation paths.
*   **Document Storage:** Upload invoices, agreements, and tax documents directly to the vendor profile.

### 4. 💳 Financial Tracking & Budgeting
Gain full visibility into your IT spend and budget allocations.
*   **Invoice Tracking:** Link POs, invoices, and payment receipts.
*   **Cost Centers:** Allocate costs to specific departments for accurate financial reporting.
*   **Renewal Cost Forecasting:** Predict upcoming renewal expenses.

### 5. 🔔 Automated Reminder Engine
Never miss a renewal or maintenance window again.
*   **Customizable Cycles:** Set reminders for 120, 90, 60, 30, 15, and 7 days prior to expiry.
*   **Multi-Channel Alerts:** Notifications via Dashboard, Email, and Push Notifications.
*   **Escalation Workflows:** Automatically escalate overdue actions to management.

### 6. 📊 Analytics & Reporting Dashboard
Make data-driven decisions with real-time insights.
*   **Key KPIs:** View Expiring Licenses, AMC Due Counts, and Vendor Spend Analysis.
*   **Asset Utilization:** Identify underutilized hardware and software to cut costs.
*   **Department-wise Allocation:** Understand where your IT budget is going.

### 7. 🔒 Enterprise-Grade Security
Built for modern enterprise compliance.
*   **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Super Admin, IT Admin, Finance, Procurement, and Read-Only Auditor roles.
*   **Audit Trails:** Every action is logged in a secure Compliance Log.
*   **Encrypted Storage:** License keys and sensitive data are securely encrypted.
