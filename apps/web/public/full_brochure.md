# AMGI Workflow: The Ultimate Enterprise Operating System

**Your Unified Platform for Project Management, HR, Collaboration, and IT Asset Control.**

---

## 🚀 Overview

**AMGI Workflow** is a comprehensive, enterprise-grade SaaS platform designed to eliminate silos across your organization. By combining Project Management, Human Resources, Real-Time Communications, and IT Asset Lifecycle tracking into a single pane of glass, AMGI empowers teams to work smarter, stay compliant, and drive productivity.

---

## 🌟 Core Modules

### 1. 📋 Advanced Project & Task Management
Take control of your deliverables with precision and clarity.
*   **Hierarchical Tasking:** Break down projects into tasks and sub-tasks with priority levels, deadlines, and estimated hours.
*   **Submission & Review Workflow:** Employees submit task proofs (documents/images) which managers can review, accept, or reject with comments.
*   **Recurring Tasks & Scheduling:** Automate repetitive work with daily, weekly, or monthly recurring tasks linked to your central calendar.
*   **Real-time Comments & Mentions:** Discuss task specifics inline with `@mentions` that trigger instant notifications.

### 2. 👥 Human Resource Management (HRM)
Empower your workforce and streamline HR operations.
*   **Attendance Tracking:** Built-in punch-in/punch-out functionality with timestamping.
*   **Leave Management:** Manage sick, casual, annual, and unpaid leave with customizable yearly balances and an automated approval workflow.
*   **Performance & Objectives (OKRs):** Track quarterly objectives and key results (OKRs) directly tied to employee performance.
*   **Skill Matrices & Onboarding:** Track employee proficiency levels and ensure smooth integration via structured onboarding checklists.

### 3. 💬 Enterprise Communications
Stay connected without leaving the platform.
*   **Real-Time Chat:** Direct messaging and dedicated project channels powered by WebSockets.
*   **Emergency Broadcasting:** Trigger organization-wide 'Emergency Events' that instantly alert all relevant personnel until resolved.
*   **Organization Notices:** Publish pinned announcements and expiry-based notices for company-wide visibility.
*   **Push Notifications:** Instant alerts for task assignments, mentions, and updates across desktop and mobile.

### 4. 📅 Centralized Calendar & Scheduling
Sync your organization's timeline.
*   **Unified View:** View deadlines, meetings, holidays, and reminders in one place.
*   **Event Attendees:** Invite users to calendar events with RSVP tracking.
*   **Color-Coded Organization:** Easily distinguish between different event types and project milestones.

### 5. 💻 IT Asset & Software License Management (SaaS)
Total control over your physical and digital infrastructure.
*   **Hardware Tracking:** Manage laptops, servers, and devices from procurement to secure disposal. Track warranties, AMCs, and current depreciated value.
*   **Software Licensing:** Monitor M365, AWS, Adobe, and more. Track seat assignments versus purchased capacity to eliminate wasted spend.
*   **Financials & Vendors:** Centralize vendor contracts, invoices, and SLA details. 
*   **Automated Expiry Alerts:** Never miss a renewal with intelligent 120/90/30/7-day reminder cycles.

### 6. 📁 Secure Document Management
A single source of truth for your files.
*   **Centralized Repository:** Upload, categorize, and link documents directly to projects or users.
*   **AI & OCR Ready:** Extract text and generate AI summaries from uploaded PDFs and images.
*   **Strict Access Control:** Documents are governed by robust Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

## 🛡️ Security & Enterprise Architecture

*   **Multi-Tenant Architecture:** Seamlessly manage multiple organizations, branches, and departments.
*   **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Granular permissions for Super Admins, Tenants, HR, Elite Users, and Interns.
*   **Audit Logging:** Every critical action (logins, deletions, status changes) is permanently recorded in the Compliance Log.
*   **Modern Integrations:** Ready to connect with Microsoft Graph, Azure MSAL, Google APIs, and Firebase.
*   **Advanced Authentication:** Support for Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA/TOTP) and seamless OAuth SSO.
